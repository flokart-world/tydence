Judges the stamps that carry a commit. From the starting commit
(HEAD, or --commit) every line of history is followed to its nearest
stamp claim, and each claim receives the full fail-closed verdict:

  1. manifest syntax and format version,
  2. bidirectional agreement between manifest and tree,
  3. full token verification — imprint, CMS signature, chain to a
     trust anchor, timestamping EKU, ESSCertID, revocation against
     the sealed CRL snapshots,
  4. renewal-chain linkage: the bound earlier artifacts recompute to
     the manifest's past-manifest / past-token hashes.

A stamp with several tokens stands as long as one token stands;
every rejected token is still reported. Anything undecidable fails.

Under each passing stamp's line, the sealed manifest's double hash
is printed exactly as the manifest grammar spells it:

  PASS <commit> (<site> at <genTime>)
    manifest sha256:<hex> sha3-256:<hex>

This is the value to transcribe into an anchor outside the
repository — a dated notarial declaration, say. It appears only on
a passed verdict, so a printed digest is always a verified one.

Earlier stamps are covered through the renewal chain (check 4) of
the stamps judged here; verifying the nearest claims is verifying
the whole line of evidence in front.

Trust anchors are supplied exactly as for stamping — see
`tydence stamp --help`. CRL snapshots and companion certificates are
taken from the sealed .tydence/ltv/ records and, when present, from
the not-yet-sealed working-tree deposits, which are then reported so
they can be sealed.

Exits 0 when at least one stamp was judged and every judged stamp
passed; 1 otherwise.
